Authoritative rules for CARD IMAGERY — the artwork that fills a card's face (its background
scene, behind the frame and above the title). App-defined; they take precedence over the
deck-style direction.

- A single, evocative illustration that fills the card's imagery area.
- It MAY depict figures, scenes, and symbolic elements appropriate to the specific card, and
  SHOULD honor traditional tarot symbolism for that card.
- Keep the focal subject centered and composed to sit within a card frame with a title strip
  along the bottom — leave a little breathing room at the edges.
- Cohesive with the deck's style and suit iconography so every card reads as one deck.
- No text, letters, or numerals anywhere in the imagery.
