Authoritative rules for the CARD BACK — the single uniform reverse shown when a card is face
down. App-defined; they take precedence over the deck-style direction.

- ONE design, identical for every card in the deck (it is the back of all of them).
- Centered and symmetric (rotationally or mirrored) so it looks the same upright or reversed.
- Rich, decorative, and evocative — this is the first image the owner sees before the flip.
- Fills the entire card edge-to-edge; cohesive with the deck style and suit iconography.
- No single-suit emblems, no court figures or scenes, and no text, letters, or numerals.
