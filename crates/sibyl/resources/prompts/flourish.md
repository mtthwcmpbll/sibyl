Authoritative rules for DECORATIVE FLOURISHES — small ornamental accents (corner pieces,
dividers) used to decorate the card layout. App-defined; they take precedence over the
deck-style direction.

- A single, small ornament that accents a corner or edge of the card.
- Simple, elegant, and scalable; stays legible at small size.
- Harmonizes with the card border and the overall deck style.
- No suit emblems, no figures, no scenes, and no text, letters, or numerals.
- Self-contained on a transparent background so it composites cleanly.
