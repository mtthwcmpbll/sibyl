Authoritative rules for the CARD BORDER & CHROME — the ornamental frame that runs along the
edges of every card. App-defined; they take precedence over the deck-style direction.

- A rectangular frame that runs along all four edges and leaves the center open for the card
  imagery (the middle must be transparent, not filled).
- Consistent, symmetric corner treatment; ornamental but not noisy; reads clearly at a glance.
- Harmonizes with the deck's suit iconography and overall style, like the metal/ink edge of a
  fine tarot card.
- No suit emblems, no figures, no scenes, and no text, letters, or numerals.
- Self-contained on a transparent background so it composites cleanly over card imagery.
